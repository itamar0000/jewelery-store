# Design System: Jewelry for Less

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Quiet Atelier"**

The storefront is a small jewellery workshop seen in daylight: warm ivory walls, one deep forest green used the way a jeweller uses a single good ink, and a Hebrew book serif set large and at regular weight. The lettering does the talking, so the headings never go bold. Photographs carry all the warmth the gold needs. They sit in arches and under one sweeping curve, never in boxes.

Density is low and the pacing is editorial. Each band of the home page is a full-width statement: hero, arches, best sellers, a green field, steps on a recessed band, a wide photograph, collections, questions. Bands get their rhythm from named section tiers and a change of ground (ivory, recessed ivory, green field), not from cards or shadows. The page closes on a green-black night footer.

The world rejects two things by name. One is the black-and-gold "luxury" shorthand. The other is the cold paper-and-ink storefront this world replaced.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Warm ivory ground, green-black ink, and forest green as the only hue of action and emphasis.
- Frank Ruhl Libre for every heading, always at regular weight; Heebo light for reading.
- Pills for every action and every chip.
- Arches frame category and collection photographs. The hero carries the page's single curve.
- One green field band with ivory type; a green-black night footer.
- Flat surfaces with hairline rules. A shadow appears only on what floats.

## Colors

The palette is warm and restrained: ivory and green-black neutrals, plus one forest green that means "act here" or "look here".

### Primary

- **Atelier Forest Green** (green): every primary pill, the selected chip, the word picked out in the hero line, the step numerals, link colour, nav underline, hover colour on serif titles, and the ground of the field band. On a hover it deepens to **Workshop Shadow Green** (green-deep).
- **Sage Wash** (green-pale): appears only on the green field, for the bullet marks and quiet details sitting on the band.

### Neutral

- **Atelier Ivory** (paper): the page ground, header, drawers and menus. It also serves as type colour on the green (primary pills, field band).
- **Recessed Ivory** (paper-deep): the recessed band (order steps, muted editorial panel), the accent badge ground and the scrollbar track.
- **Green-Black Ink** (ink): all primary type, the focus ring, the scrim (at alpha), and the night footer ground.
- **Soft Ink** (ink-soft): secondary prose on long reading lines, such as the feature banner and editorial lists.
- **Muted Ink** (ink-muted): metadata, intros under headings, inactive nav and icon controls.
- **Hairline** (line): dividers, the header rule, FAQ rows and chip outlines.
- **Strong Hairline** (line-strong): the outlines of controls (field underlines, checkboxes) at 3:1, link underline decoration, and text selection.
- **Night Fog** (night-foreground): body type on the night footer. Footer headings and hovers use ivory.

### Status

- **Danger** (danger), **Success** (success), **Warning** (warning): desaturated toward ink, used only in forms and order states.

### Named Rules

**The One Hue Rule.** Green is the only hue of action and emphasis. Hover, active, selected, link and primary all resolve to green or ink, never to a second colour. Any other warmth on a page comes from the photographs.

**The Field Rule.** The green field is a band of forest green with ivory type. The action on it is the inverse pill (ivory, green type), never a green pill.

**The Night Rule.** The footer is the night ground (ink) with Night Fog type. No other band uses the night ground.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Frank Ruhl Libre (with David, Frank Ruehl CLM, Times New Roman, serif)
**Body Font:** Heebo (with Segoe UI, system-ui, Noto Sans Hebrew, Arial, sans-serif)

**Character:** A century-old Hebrew book serif, set large at regular weight, over a clean, open sans at 300. The serif's stroke contrast carries the hierarchy, so the body never needs a third weight.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (400; 2.5rem on phones, 4.5rem from sm, clamp(3.5rem, 5.6vw, 5.75rem) from lg; line-height 1.02 to 1.04; -0.01em): the hero line only. One word may be set in green.
- **Headline** (400; 3rem, then 3.75rem from md; feature banner reaches 4.5rem at xl, the closing band 4.5rem from md; line-height 1.1): section headings, text-balanced.
- **Numeral** (400, 2.5rem, line-height 1, green): order-step numbers.
- **Wordmark** (400, Latin, 0.02em): "Jewelry for Less" in the serif - 1.375rem in the header, 1.625rem from lg, 2rem in the night footer. Its own step: the name is a mark, not a heading.
- **Title Large** (400, 1.75rem): collection names under the wide arches.
- **Title** (400, 1.375rem): category names under arches, FAQ questions.
- **Title Small** (400, 1.25rem, snug): product names on cards.
- **Lead** (Heebo 300, 1.125rem, 1.8): the hero sentence and feature prose, at most 34ch in the hero and 30em elsewhere.
- **Body** (Heebo 300, 1rem, 1.8): section intros and prose.
- **Body Small** (Heebo 300 to 500, 0.9375rem): button labels at medium size, prices (500, tabular), editorial list items and step descriptions.
- **Label** (Heebo 500, 0.875rem): footer column heads, nav items, chips and small buttons.
- **Caption** (Heebo 400, 0.75rem): metadata, badges and legal lines. 0.75rem is the floor; there is no smaller step.

### Named Rules

**The Regular Serif Rule.** Serif headings are set at regular weight (400), never bold or semibold. If a heading needs more weight, make it larger.

**The Untracked Hebrew Rule.** Hebrew never takes positive letter-spacing. Tracking runs from tight (-0.02em) to normal only. The Latin wordmark is the single exception (0.02em).

**The No Kicker Rule.** No eyebrow or kicker labels above headings. A section opens with its serif headline.

## Layout

Mobile-first, with min-width breakpoints at 40, 48, 64, 80 and 96rem. Content sits in a 72rem container, catalogue grids run to 90rem, forms and the FAQ to 48rem, and prose to 40rem with a 30em reading measure. Spacing derives from a 0.25rem unit. Vertical rhythm uses four named tiers: tight (3rem) for a rail under a band, section (4.5rem) for the default and for most mobile sections, feature (7rem) for a band that stops the scroll, and finale (9rem) for the closing statement.

On desktop the hero is two columns filling the first screen below the header. Text sits at the inline start (right, in RTL) over ivory, and the photograph fills the other column at 1.15fr. On phones the photograph comes first at 60svh. A `short:` variant (height under 50rem) tightens only the hero. Categories run as a 2/3/5-column grid of arches. Editorial panels split into two equal halves from md. The steps run 2 columns, then 4 from lg, under a strong hairline.

## Elevation & Depth

The world is flat. Depth comes from a change of ground (ivory, recessed ivory, green field, night) and from hairlines. Photographs sit flush, and product packshots are seated into the ivory with a darken blend, so the studio white disappears into the page ground. Shadows appear only on things that float above the page.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Header lift** (`box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgb(6 20 34 / 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgb(6 20 34 / 0.05)`): the sticky header, only once the page has scrolled.
- **Menu** (`box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -6px rgb(6 20 34 / 0.1), 0 4px 8px -4px rgb(6 20 34 / 0.06)`): the mega menu.
- **Drawer** (`box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgb(6 20 34 / 0.13)`): the mobile navigation drawer.

### Named Rules

**The Flush Photograph Rule.** No shadow, frame or card under a photograph. A product image sits directly on the ivory.

## Shapes

The page uses three shapes, each with its own job. **Pills** (fully round) are for actions and chips. **Arches** are for photographs that open a choice: category arches are fully round-topped at 3:4, and collection arches are 3:2 with a 10rem top radius, widening to 14rem from md. **The curve** is the hero photograph's outer lower corner, swept at 30vw on phones and 40vw from lg. Everything else is square: product images, editorial halves, the feature banner, the header and fields. Small status badges take a 0.125rem corner. Colour swatches are circles.

**The One Curve Rule.** A page carries one sweeping curve, and on the home page that is the hero. Arches are a separate device and do not count as curves. They belong to categories and collections.

## Components

### Buttons

Calm, fully round tags.

- **Shape:** pill (9999px); medium weight; sizes 2.25rem / 3rem / 3.5rem high with 1rem / 1.75rem / 2.25rem inline padding.
- **Primary:** forest green with ivory type, deepening to green-deep on hover. Use one per view.
- **Secondary:** 1px outline in the current colour with inherited type, so it is correct on any ground. Hover adds a 5% ink wash. This is the default variant.
- **Inverse:** ivory with green type. Use it only on the green field.
- **Link:** green text with a strong-hairline underline at 0.35em offset, which turns green on hover.
- **Focus:** the global 2px outline at 2px offset, in ink. On the night footer and the field band, the ring switches to ivory.

### Chips

- **Style:** pills, 2.25rem to 2.75rem high, with a hairline outline and ink type (subcategory nav, filter triggers, PDP options, pagination).
- **State:** selected is solid green with ivory type. Hover darkens the outline to strong hairline over the recessed ivory.

### Badges

- **Style:** small tags (0.125rem corner, caption size, medium weight). The accent tone is green type on recessed ivory, set at the card image's top inline-start corner. A card carries at most one badge.

### Cards / Containers

- **Corner Style:** none. Product cards have no frame.
- **Background:** the page ivory. The packshot is seated with a darken blend.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Content:** a serif name (Title Small), a caption meta line, a medium-weight tabular price, and swatch circles. The hover image cross-fades over 700ms, or a single image zooms 4% over 1400ms.

### Inputs / Fields

- **Style:** an underline field. A strong-hairline bottom border on a transparent ground, 2.75rem high.
- **Focus:** the underline turns green. The global focus ring also applies.

### Navigation

- **Header:** an ivory sticky bar with a hairline bottom rule. On desktop, a 4rem masthead row sits over a ruled 3rem navigation row. The Latin wordmark is in the serif.
- **Nav items:** Label size in muted ink, turning to ink on hover. The active or open item takes a 1px green underline that scales in from the centre.
- **Mobile:** an ivory drawer from the inline start with a drawer shadow.

### Signature: Hero Curve

A two-column first screen. Its parts are the serif display line with one green word, one Heebo lead sentence, and two pills (primary "catalogue", secondary "custom"). The photograph's outer lower corner is swept by the 40vw curve. The photograph settles from a 4% over-scale over 2s, and the copy rises in with an 80ms stagger.

### Signature: Arches

The categories run as five round-topped 3:4 arches with centred serif names. The collections use wide 3:2 arches with Title Large names and a green underlined link. On hover, the image zooms 3 to 4% over 1400ms on the settle curve.

### Signature: Field Band

The editorial split with the field tone. One half is a photograph and the other is forest green, with ivory type, sage-wash bullets and an inverse pill.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** use forest green (green) as the only hue of action and emphasis, and green-deep for its hover.
- **Do** set every heading in Frank Ruhl Libre at weight 400 and let size carry the hierarchy.
- **Do** make every action and every chip a pill (9999px).
- **Do** frame category and collection photographs in arches, and keep the sweeping curve for the hero alone.
- **Do** put ivory type and the inverse pill on the green field, and Night Fog type on the night footer.
- **Do** pace bands with the named section tiers (3 / 4.5 / 7 / 9rem) and changes of ground, not with boxes.
- **Do** seat packshots into the ivory with a darken blend.

### Don't:

- **Don't** add eyebrow or kicker labels above headings.
- **Don't** letter-space Hebrew positively or set it in uppercase-style tracking.
- **Don't** bold a serif heading.
- **Don't** introduce a second accent hue, gold type, or black-and-gold "luxury" styling.
- **Don't** put a second sweeping curve on a page.
- **Don't** place a shadow, frame or card behind a product photograph.
- **Don't** set type below 0.75rem.
