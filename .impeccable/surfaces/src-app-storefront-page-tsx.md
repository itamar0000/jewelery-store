# Storefront home — surface brief

Scope: the storefront home page and the visual world it establishes for every
other surface. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

Audience: Hebrew-reading buyers in Israel, in three roughly equal situations
(bridal, gift, self-purchase) — no primary funnel. Job: believe this workshop
can make the piece they want, properly. Action: open the catalogue. Proof on
hand: real product and editorial photography only. Constraints: prices are
placeholders, no testimonial has been collected, no physical shop is confirmed,
no logo exists; the shop sells 14K only.

Chosen by the owner (2026-10-07) from five rendered alternatives in
`docs/design-alternatives/`: **A, "סטודיו" (Atelier)**. The previous world,
paper and ink only, is replaced. `docs/design-alternatives/a-atelier.html` is
the critique reference.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** A quiet jewellery atelier: warm ivory, deep forest green, a Hebrew
book serif at display size, and photographs framed in arches and a single
sweeping curve. It refuses the black-and-gold "luxury" shorthand and the cold
paper-and-ink storefront it replaces.

**OWN-WORLD.** Ground ivory #f4efe6, recessed band #ebe4d6, ink #1d2a24 (green
black), forest green #2f4a3c as the one colour of action and emphasis, muted
#6b6f66, hairline #d6cdbb. Frank Ruhl Libre for every heading, Heebo light for
reading. Pill buttons: filled green, or a 1px ink outline. Arches on category
photographs, one 40vw curve on the hero photograph. A green field band with
ivory type; a green-black footer.

**STORY.** The visitor reads that each piece is made for them, directly from
the workshop, with nothing between; sees the five categories as arches; sees
what is ordered most; understands made-to-order in four steps; opens the
catalogue or starts a custom request.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** Desktop: two columns, text at the inline start (right)
over ivory — the display line in serif at up to 92px with one word in green,
one sentence of Heebo, two pill actions (green "catalogue", outlined "custom")
— and the hero photograph filling the other column to the viewport's bottom,
its outer lower corner swept by a 40vw curve. Phone: photograph first, 60vh,
curved; the line beneath.

**FORM.** Owner-pinned direction A (outside the roll); seed key 5148482c,
challengers declined as unrelated to a jewellery shop.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
